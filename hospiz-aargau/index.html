Context & Market Development

Today, the tomorrow of Hospiz Aargau is being decided.

Hospices today carry more weight than ever before. Rising costs, shrinking public funding, and a donor generation that gives almost exclusively online are changing everything.

This generation expects more than just a donate button. They want instant online giving, personal stories and the feeling of really making an impact. This isn't a trend – it's the new standard.

Whoever actively shapes this shift today builds stability for the coming years. Whoever waits loses 60–70 % of potential donors before they ever give.

Three key observations

Three brakes costing you donors.

These aren't minor cosmetic tweaks. They're three structural issues that are closely connected – and each one is costing you donors, donations and reputation. The good news: all three can be systematically fixed.

01
Observation

Outdated website.

Your current website was built years ago and now lags significantly behind the standard of hospice organizations in the DACH region. Modern donors expect speed, clear mobile experience, strong trust signals and emotional impact stories. An outdated site subconsciously lowers the perception of the entire organization – before the visitor even sees the donation form.

Impact ★★★★★
→ Solution: new, conversion-optimized website
02
Observation

Poor donation form.

The current donation form doesn't guide the donor – it just waits for them. There are no concrete impact amounts (e.g. „CHF 80 = one day of hospice care“), recurring giving isn't suggested as the default and the donor journey is fragmented. This single page decides whether the visitor completes their donation – or leaves.

Impact ★★★★★
→ Solution: donation flow optimization + redesign
03
Observation

Absence of paid ads.

In the Meta Ads Library, I found no paid campaigns by Hospiz Aargau. Your Facebook page has only 225 fans after 30 years of existence – meaning complete dependence on organic reach. Targeted Meta campaigns within the canton of Aargau are today one of the most effective channels to acquire new donors and strengthen the hospice's reputation.

Impact ★★★★☆
→ Solution: long-term partnership with paid advertising

Three concrete Quick Wins that make the biggest difference at Hospiz Aargau.

TWINT as the leading donation method

In Switzerland, donors decide in seconds today. Whoever doesn't offer TWINT prominently and optimally loses a large share of impulses.

Quick Win

Prominent TWINT button on all emotional story pages, with predefined amounts (50 / 100 / 200 CHF).

Recurring donations as a strategic priority

A hospice needs predictable revenue more than one-off campaign successes.

Quick Win

Highlight monthly giving as the primary option – with a clear, dignified rationale:

“With CHF 8–15 monthly, you accompany a person with dignity to their last breath.”

Frictionless mobile donation flow

Quick Win
