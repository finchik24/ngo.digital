Context & Market Development

Today, the tomorrow of Hospiz Aargau is decided.

Hospices today face greater challenges than ever before. Rising costs, shrinking public funding, and a donor generation that gives almost exclusively online are changing everything.

This generation expects more than just a donate button. They want instant online giving, personal stories and the feeling of really making an impact. This isn't a trend – it's the new standard.

Whoever actively shapes this today secures stability. Studies show: 60–70 % of mobile visitors drop off when the donation process isn't optimized – before they ever give.

Three key observations

Three brakes costing you donors.

These aren't minor cosmetic tweaks. They're three structural issues that are closely connected – and each one is costing you donors, donations and reputation. The good news: all three can be systematically fixed.

01
Observation

Outdated website.

Your current website was built years ago and now lags significantly behind the standard of hospice organizations in the DACH region. Modern donors expect speed, clear mobile experience, strong trust signals and emotional impact stories. An outdated site subconsciously lowers the perception of the entire organization – before the visitor even sees the donation form.

Impact ★★★★★
→ Solution: new, conversion-optimized website
02
Observation

A donation page without a donation option.

I analyzed your donation page – and had to look twice. There is not a single online donation option. No TWINT, no credit card, no Apple Pay, no PayPal. Just an IBAN number and a PDF to download.

For 80 % of your potential donors aged 30 to 55, this is the end of the donation attempt. They come from a smartphone, they have 30 seconds, they want to give – but your page asks them to download a PDF, scan a QR code in their banking app and transfer manually. Almost no one does that anymore.

On top of that: there are no concrete impact amounts, no story, no invitation to monthly giving. The page talks about Hospiz Aargau – not about the donor and not about the person being helped.

This single page decides whether the visitor donates – or leaves. Right now they leave.

Impact ★★★★★
→ Solution: complete redesign of the donation process with TWINT, Apple Pay, monthly giving as default and concrete impact amounts
03
Observation

Absence of paid ads.

In the Meta Ads Library, I found no paid campaigns by Hospiz Aargau. Your Facebook page has only 615 followers after 30 years of existence – meaning complete dependence on organic reach.

With a monthly budget of 500–800 CHF you could run targeted campaigns in the canton of Aargau that reach hundreds of new donors – with full measurable control over cost per donation and ROI.

Targeted Meta campaigns within the canton of Aargau are today one of the most effective channels to acquire new donors and strengthen the hospice's reputation. Other hospices in the DACH region are using exactly this lever – with measurable success.

Impact ★★★★☆
→ Solution: starting with a targeted test campaign in the canton of Aargau

TWINT as the leading donation method

In Switzerland, donors decide in seconds today. Whoever doesn't offer TWINT optimally loses a large share of donors.

Quick Win

Prominent TWINT button on all emotional story pages, with predefined amounts (50 / 100 / 200 CHF).

Expected impact

Noticeably more instant donations from mobile.

02

Recurring donations as a strategic priority

A hospice needs predictable revenue more than one-off campaign successes.

Quick Win

Highlight monthly giving as the primary option – with a clear, dignified rationale:

“With CHF 8–15 monthly, you accompany a person with dignity to their last breath.”

Expected impact

More predictable income through recurring donations with strong long-term value.

03

Frictionless mobile donation flow

Over 70 % of donation visits come from mobile. Every unnecessary hurdle costs a donation.

Quick Win

Reduce the donation process to two steps maximum, prefill TWINT & Apple Pay, and a personal thank-you page with a direct offer for monthly support.

Expected impact

Higher completion rates and significantly better donor retention.
