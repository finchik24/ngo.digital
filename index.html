About me

I help nonprofits
across Europe raise
real money — not
just engagement.

I'm Peter Chalupiansky — a fundraising consultant based in Prague. For the past ten years, I've worked with nonprofits across Europe, helping them turn digital donations into a sustainable revenue channel.

My work spans two fronts. As CSR & ESG lead at Kaufland Czech Republic — part of Schwarz Gruppe, Europe's largest retailer — I've built the Christmas campaign for Světluška into a €4.9M fundraising engine over seven years. As an independent consultant, I run digital fundraising campaigns for European nonprofits — most recently the most successful Christmas campaign in Czechia of 2024.

My campaigns consistently deliver positive ROI and grow donor bases that stick.

Now I want to bring all of that to your nonprofit — and help you build a digital fundraising channel that actually works.
